# Astor site: home, /deal, /proof

- **Scope:** all three routes share this world.
  - `/` makes the case.
  - `/deal` covers the rates, the contract and the calculator.
  - `/proof` shows the dashboards and case studies.
- **Visitor mode:** Persuade.
- **Audience and action:** a creator, usually on a phone, who should book a 30-minute call.
- **Proof:** real dashboards, published rates, the 10/01 census.
- **Constraints:**
  - The black-and-gold palette is pinned by the user.
  - The copy stays largely as is.
  - Locus screens keep Locus's own look.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** Astor reaches a creator the way their money does, on the lock screen. The first screen is an iPhone lock screen: the headline is the clock, the stats are widgets, the receipts arrive as notifications, and booking is the swipe. Further down the page, every section borrows a native iPhone surface: widgets, grouped lists, a Messages thread and an incoming call. It refuses the category default of a centred serif headline with a gold phrase, two pills and a stat strip.
- **OWN-WORLD:**
  - **Palette:** warm black #090807 wallpaper lit by ember (#6e2440) and gold light from below. Gold foil (#8f7137 → #f2dda9 → #d2ac61) is reserved for the clock and key figures. Ink is #f7f2e7.
  - **Type:** Italiana (thin fashion display) for the clock, titles and big figures. Jost for text, and spaced Jost caps for labels and buttons.
  - **Surfaces:** frosted warm-glass notification cards and widgets with 22px continuous corners. Grouped inset lists. Foil pill buttons.
- **STORY:**
  1. The visitor sees the cut is smaller and that the numbers are real.
  2. They believe the terms, the rates and the exit are published and true.
  3. They book the call.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:**
  - A status bar: the ASTOR wordmark on the left; Proof, The Deal and a glass "Book your call" pill on the right.
  - Centred, the date line reads "A different type of management." in spaced caps.
  - Below it, the clock: "We take a smaller cut" in foil Italiana at about 9vw, with "than the teams we outperform." under it.
  - Then four widgets with the headline stats.
  - Then a notification stack: the intro from Astor, then a Locus earnings receipt.
  - A dock: Proof and The Deal as round corner buttons, and a foil "Book your call" pill in the centre above a home-indicator bar.
  - Signature motion: the screen wakes. The wallpaper light rises, the clock resolves from blur, and the notifications arrive one by one, springing up.
- **FORM:** The Lock Screen grammar (re-roll 1's pick), now dressed as Silk (10/05): matte black, champagne hairlines, Gilda Display headline with Jost figures, and one gold silk ribbon drawn in behind the receipts. The user steered round 3 away from velvet, Deco, ledger and foil as pompous and chose Silk from three quieter takes. Seed key 77fed2da.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
